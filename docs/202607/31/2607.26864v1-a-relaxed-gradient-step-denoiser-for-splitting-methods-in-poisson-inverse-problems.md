## 摘要
即插即用方法将经典变分模型与学习型去噪器相结合，在成像逆问题中取得了优异结果。其收敛性已在高斯数据下得到广泛研究，而泊松模型由于非二次的Kullback-Leibler保真项需要额外关注。本文引入了\gsdsplit，一种基于\pnpsplit构建的分裂方法，其中通用去噪块被替换为松弛梯度步长去噪器。所得方法保留了显式的泊松保真更新。该去噪器通过由输入凸神经网络参数化的学习型凸势函数的梯度来定义。其架构和训练同时促进了盲去噪能力和足以保证强非扩张性的平滑条件。经验平滑度估计被用于选择与\pnpsplit收敛假设兼容的松弛参数。数值实验表明，所训练的去噪器在验证数据上以FNE兼容模式运行。\gsdsplit在经验支持范围之外也能产生稳定的重建，并且对ADMM参数的敏感性显著降低，同时对不同模糊算子和泊松噪声水平仍然有效。

## Abstract
Plug\&Play methods combine classical variational models with learned denoisers and have achieved strong results in imaging inverse problems. Their convergence has been widely studied for Gaussian data, whereas Poisson models require additional care because of the nonquadratic Kullback--Leibler fidelity. This work introduces \gsdsplit, a splitting method built upon \pnpsplit, in which the generic denoising block is replaced by a relaxed Gradient Step Denoiser. The resulting method retains explicit Poisson fidelity updates. The denoiser is defined through the gradient of a learned convex potential parameterized by an Input Convex Neural Network. Its architecture and training promote both blind denoising capability and a smoothness condition sufficient for firm nonexpansiveness. Empirical smoothness estimates are used to select a relaxation parameter compatible with the convergence assumptions of \pnpsplit. Numerical experiments provide evidence that the trained denoiser operates in an FNE-compatible regime on the considered validation data. \gsdsplit also yields stable reconstructions outside the empirically supported range and shows substantially lower sensitivity to the ADMM parameter, while remaining effective for different blur operators and Poisson noise levels.
